## Getting Started with Realm

This guide will help you get up and running with Realm quickly.

### Access Realm

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log in to Realm">
    Navigate to your organization's Realm instance at `https://[your-organization].withrealm.com` and sign in with your company credentials.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Explore the interface">
    Once logged in, you'll see the main Realm interface with:
    - **Chat** - Ask questions about your company's knowledge
    - **Search** - Find documents across all connected sources
    - **Agents** - Access and create custom AI assistants
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ask your first question">
    In the Chat interface, try asking a question about your company's knowledge. For example:
    - "What is our company's vacation policy?"
    - "Show me the latest sales deck"
    - "What were the key decisions from last week's team meeting?"
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Using the @ Keybind

The `@` key is your universal shortcut in Realm:

- **Switch assistants** - Type `@` to quickly select different AI models or custom agents
- **Reference documents** - Use `@` to attach specific files to your query
- **Access tools** - Select specific tools and integrations
