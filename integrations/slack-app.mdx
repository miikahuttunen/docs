## Realm Slack App

The Realm Slack App lets you ask questions from Chat directly in Slack—just like messaging a colleague.

## Finding the Slack App

You'll find the Realm Slack App in the **"Apps"** section in Slack.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/help-center/slack-apps-section.png" alt="Realm in Slack Apps section" />
</Frame>

### Assistant Sidebar

Admins can also make the Realm Slack app available in the Slack assistants sidebar. This enables you to open Realm Slackbot from the top-right.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/help-center/slack-assistant-sidebar.png" alt="Realm in Slack assistant sidebar" />
</Frame>

<Note>
If this is not enabled, ask your admins to follow [Slack's instructions for displaying AI apps](https://slack.com/intl/en-gb/help/articles/33077521383059-Display-AI-apps-in-Slack).
</Note>

## How to Use

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Realm App">
    Find Realm in your Slack Apps section and open a direct message.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ask a Question">
    Send a message just like you would to a colleague.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get Your Answer">
    Realm will search your company knowledge and respond.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ask Follow-ups">
    Post follow-up questions in the same thread as the reply.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Using Agents in Slack Channels

You can also ask questions from different Assistants in Slack channels. For more information, see [Adding Agents to Slack Channels](/integrations/slack-agents).

## Tips for Best Results

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Be Specific" icon="bullseye">
    Include relevant context in your questions for better answers.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Use Threads for Follow-ups" icon="comments">
    Keep related questions in the same thread to maintain context.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Start Fresh for New Topics" icon="plus">
    Begin a new conversation when switching to unrelated topics.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
