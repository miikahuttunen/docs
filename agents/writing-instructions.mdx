## How to Write Good Instructions for Agents

The quality of your Agent's responses depends heavily on the instructions you provide. Follow these core principles to create effective Agents.

## Core Principles

### 1. Be Clear and Precise

Provide specific objectives and detailed instructions. Longer prompts with relevant context outperform short queries.

<Tip>
Use action verbs, define expected output format and length, and specify your target audience.
</Tip>

### 2. Provide Appropriate Context

Supply sufficient background information instead of simplifying complex topics. Modern AI models can process complex information effectively. Summarize what you want the assistant to know and do.

### 3. Structure Your Prompts Effectively

Follow a logical ordering:
1. Task context/role assignment
2. Tone setting
3. Background information/documents
4. Detailed task description with rules

### 4. Include Format Guidance

Adding examples to prompts significantly improves model response quality, especially when you need specific formatting.

<Note>
**Show, don't just tell.** Examples of desired outputs work better than descriptions of what you want.
</Note>

### 5. Define a Clear Persona

Always define a persona to set the tone of the response. This guides how the model communicates and is especially important for customer-facing documentation.

```text
Example: "You are a helpful IT support specialist who explains technical
concepts in simple terms..."
```

### 6. Tailor to Your Audience

Include specific details about your target audience to ensure relevant outputs.

```text
Example: "This content is for tech-savvy developers at a startup who are
familiar with REST APIs..."
```

### 7. Address Potential Issues Proactively

Provide instructions for handling edge cases. Focus on reliably covering base cases first before moving to edge cases.

### 8. Test and Iterate

Evaluate prompts under various scenarios and provide feedback to improve outputs. Prompt engineering is an iterative process.

## Example Instruction Structure

```text
# Role
You are [specific role] for [company/context].

# Task
Given [input], your job is to [specific task].

# Guidelines
- [Guideline 1]
- [Guideline 2]
- [Guideline 3]

# Output Format
[Describe expected format]

# Examples
Input: [example input]
Output: [example output]

# Constraints
- Only use information from the provided materials
- If unsure, say so rather than guessing
- [Other constraints]
```

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

<Warning>
**Don't:**
- Use vague instructions like "be helpful"
- Assume the AI knows your company context
- Give contradictory instructions
- Make instructions too long without structure
</Warning>
