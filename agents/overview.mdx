## What are Agents?

Think of Agents as "mini-programs" with a specific task (e.g., email writing, POC doc creation, IT help desk).

Agents can:
- Access a specific set of internal knowledge
- Perform web searches
- Follow your specific instructions
- Integrate with your workflows (e.g., Slack, HubSpot, Gmail)

## Example Agents

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Translation Assistant" icon="language">
    **Purpose:** Helps with different translation tasks

    **Knowledge:** No company context—works with any text

    **Instructions:**
    > You are an expert in language and communication. Your task is to translate text between different languages accurately and efficiently. Maintain the original tone and style, ensure cultural appropriateness, and use correct grammar and spelling in the target language.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Customer Bot Assistant" icon="users">
    **Purpose:** Writes summaries on accounts when given a customer name

    **Knowledge:** Specific folders and channels related to customers

    **Instructions:**
    > You are a digital assistant for summarizing customer information. Given a customer name, summarize everything you know including: tools connected, contract start date, pricing, meeting history, key contacts, and other useful information. Use only the provided contextual materials.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Finding Agents

There are multiple ways to access Agents:

### From the Sidebar
Browse the Agent gallery from the sidebar and click "Talk" to start a conversation.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/help-center/agent-gallery.png" alt="Agent gallery in sidebar" />
</Frame>

### Using the @ Keybind
In Chat, type `@` to see your available Agents and select the one you want to use.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/help-center/at-agents.png" alt="Selecting agents with @ keybind" />
</Frame>

### Pin Favorites
On the Chat page, you can pin your favorite Agents (up to 6) for quick access.

  <img src="/images/help-center/pinned-agents.png" alt="Pinned agents" />
