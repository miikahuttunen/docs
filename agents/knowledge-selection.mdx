## Selecting Knowledge for Agents

When creating an Agent with access to specific knowledge, you'll configure what sources it can search through.

## Knowledge Access Options

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="All Company Knowledge" icon="building">
    Access to everything connected to Realm
  </Card>
  <Card title="Selected Knowledge" icon="folder-open">
    Only specific files, folders, or sources you choose
  </Card>
  <Card title="No Knowledge" icon="lock">
    Pure AI without company context
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Selecting Specific Knowledge

When you choose to give your Agent access to specific knowledge, you'll use the Knowledge view.


### What You Can Select

- **Individual files** from any connected source
- **Folders** from Google Drive, SharePoint, etc.
- **Channels** from Slack
- **Spaces** from Confluence
- **Projects** from Jira
- **Uploaded files** specifically for this Agent

## Uploading Files for Agents

You can upload files specifically for your Agent to use. These files will be part of the Agent's knowledge base.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Knowledge View">
    Navigate to the Knowledge selection screen while configuring your Agent.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload Files">
    Click the upload button to add files from your device.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm Selection">
    The uploaded files will appear in your selected knowledge.
  </Step>
</Steps>


## Best Practices

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Be Selective" icon="filter">
    Only include knowledge that's relevant to your Agent's purpose. Too much irrelevant data can reduce accuracy.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Keep Sources Updated" icon="rotate">
    If you upload files, remember to update them when the underlying information changes.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Use Verified Content" icon="badge-check">
    When possible, include documents that have been [verified](/curation/verifying-and-hiding) in Realm.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Test with Different Queries" icon="vial">
    After setting up knowledge, test your Agent with various questions to ensure it's finding the right information.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
