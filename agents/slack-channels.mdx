## Agents in Slack Channels

Realm allows you to add Agents to Slack channels. For example, if you have a `#solutions-engineering` Slack channel, you could add a Virtual Solutions Engineer Agent there.

Your team can then ask questions in the Slack channel by writing `@Realm` and asking their question.

## Setting Up an Agent in a Channel

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add Realm to the Channel">
    Type `@Realm` in the channel you'd like to connect your Agent to, then add Realm when prompted.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy the Channel ID">
    In the channel, click on the channel name at the top, then scroll to the bottom to find the channel ID.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure the Agent">
    When configuring your Agent, find the Slack section at the bottom.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the Channel">
    Paste your Slack channel ID and click "Add Channel".
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How It Works

Once configured:

1. Team members type `@Realm` in the channel followed by their question
2. The configured Agent processes the question
3. The Agent responds with an answer, drawing from its specific knowledge and instructions

## Example Use Cases

| Channel | Agent Purpose |
|---------|---------------|
| `#sales-support` | Answer questions about products, pricing, and competitors |
| `#it-helpdesk` | Help with common IT issues and procedures |
| `#customer-success` | Provide information about customer accounts and history |
| `#hr-questions` | Answer questions about company policies and benefits |

## Best Practices

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Match Agent to Channel Purpose" icon="bullseye">
    Configure the Agent's knowledge and instructions to align with the channel's topic.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Curate Knowledge Carefully" icon="folder">
    Only include relevant knowledge sources to ensure accurate, focused answers.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Set Clear Instructions" icon="clipboard">
    Write instructions that help the Agent understand the context of questions in that channel.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Inform Your Team" icon="bullhorn">
    Let channel members know about the Agent and how to use it effectively.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Limitations

<Note>
Only users with an active Realm license will be able to use the Realm Assistant in Slack channels.
</Note>
