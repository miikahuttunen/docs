## AI Models in Realm

Realm supports leading AI models that you can use directly in Chat for general-purpose reasoning and writing.

## Available Models

| Model | Provider | Web Access | Best For |
|-------|----------|------------|----------|
| **GPT-5** | OpenAI | Yes | The best general-purpose model by OpenAI |
| **GPT-4.1** | OpenAI | Yes | An older general-purpose model by OpenAI |
| **Claude 4.5 Sonnet** | Anthropic | Yes | Excels at coding, technical projects, and copywriting |
| **Gemini 2.5 Flash** | Google | Yes | A very fast model by Google |
| **Gemini 2.5 Pro** | Google | Yes | The best general-purpose model by Google |
| **o3** | OpenAI | Yes | Deep thinking for math, SQL, and complex problems |
| **o4-mini** | OpenAI | Yes | Balanced speed and intelligence for everyday tasks |

<Note>
If you don't see all AI models in Realm, your admin may not have enabled them in your workspace. Contact your administrator to enable additional models.
</Note>

## How to Use AI Models

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Chat">
    Start a new chat or open an existing conversation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a Model">
    Type `@` in the chat input, or click the Realm icon.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Your Model">
    Select the AI model you want to use from the list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start Chatting">
    Ask your question or provide your prompt.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Web Search Capability

All AI models in Realm can search online for you. This is a seamless process—Realm automatically decides from your query if an online search is necessary.

### Example Use Cases

- "What's the latest news about [company]?"
- "Find recent research on [topic]"
- "What are the current best practices for [technology]?"

## Model Selection Tips

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Speed vs. Quality" icon="gauge">
