## What is Realm?

Realm is a platform for searching, asking questions, and building custom AI agents on top of your company's knowledge. It includes built-in tools for tasks like answering RFPs, conducting web searches, and leveraging leading language models—out of the box.

## Who's behind Realm?

Realm is a startup based in Helsinki, Finland, founded by former engineers from Pipe, Stripe, and Dropbox, as well as former Slush executives. Realm is backed by the founders of Zalando, Supercell, Wolt, Snyk, Pigment, Aiven, and others.
