## What is Search?

Realm has a workplace search engine that works across all platforms you've connected. Think of it as a quick and easy way to find what you need to do your work—documents, presentations, messages—just like Google Search for your company.

## When to Use Search

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Find Exact Files" icon="file-search">
    You have a specific file in mind and want to open it quickly
  </Card>
  <Card title="Locate Forgotten Documents" icon="lightbulb">
    You want to revisit a document but don't remember exactly what it's called
  </Card>
  <Card title="Get the Big Picture" icon="binoculars">
    Quickly scroll through every mention of a customer, product feature, or topic
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## How Search Works

Realm searches across all document information—not just titles. Unlike many search bars in your workspace, Realm's results are based on much more than a simple keyword search.

### Search Techniques

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Keyword Matching" icon="text">
    Traditional keyword search finds exact matches in your documents.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Semantic Matching" icon="brain">
    Understands the meaning of your query, even when you don't remember the exact words.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Relevance Signals" icon="signal">
    Results are ranked by freshness, sharing frequency, and verification status.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Search Suggestions

As you type your search in Realm, you'll see suggestions appear.

<Note>
**Important:** Suggestions are based on **title only** and **do not include Slack messages**. For a complete search experience, press Enter to run the search and view results on the Search Results page.
</Note>

## Improving Search Results
