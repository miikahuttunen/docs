## Filtering Results

Realm offers five filters that help you narrow down your search. You can apply as many at the same time as you wish.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/help-center/search-filters.png" alt="Search filter options" />
</Frame>

### Available Filters

| Filter | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **From** | Show results from specific authors (the person who created a document, sent a message, or left a comment) |
| **Created** | Show results created within a certain time range |
| **Updated** | Show results last updated within a certain time range |
| **Type** | Show results from specific file types like PDFs, Issues, or Spreadsheets |
| **App** | Show results from a specific connected app |

## How to Apply Filters

<Steps>
  <Step title="Run Your Search">
    Enter your search query and press Enter.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Filter Panel">
    Click on the filter options above your search results.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Filter Values">
    Choose the criteria you want to filter by.
  </Step>
  <Step title="View Filtered Results">
    Results update automatically as you apply filters.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Sorting Results

By default, the most relevant results are shown first. However, you can also sort results by:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Last Edited" icon="clock-rotate-left">
    The most recently updated results are shown first
  </Card>
  <Card title="Last Created" icon="calendar-plus">
    The most recently created results are shown first
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Combining Filters

You can combine multiple filters for precise results:

```text
Example: Find all PDF documents created by Sarah in the last month from Google Drive
- Type: PDF
- From: Sarah
- Created: Last 30 days
- App: Google Drive
```

## Tips for Effective Filtering

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Start Broad, Then Narrow" icon="filter">
    Begin with a general search, then apply filters to narrow down results.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Use Time Filters for Recent Content" icon="calendar">
    When looking for recent discussions or updates, use the Created or Updated filters.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Combine App and Type Filters" icon="layer-group">
    Filter by both app and document type to find specific file formats from particular sources.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
