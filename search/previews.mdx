## Document Preview

You can preview most search results right inside Realm by clicking **Open Preview**. This way, you often don't have to open another tab to find what you're looking for.


### Supported Preview Types

- Documents (PDFs, Word, Google Docs)
- Spreadsheets (Excel, Google Sheets)
- Presentations (PowerPoint, Google Slides)
- Messages (Slack, Teams)
- Wiki pages (Confluence, Notion)
- And more

## Turn into Chat

With the **Turn into Chat** feature, you can seamlessly convert a search result into a conversation to ask follow-up questions and explore the topic further.

---

## Chat with Document

You can also talk directly to a specific document.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Chat with Document">
    From the document preview or search result, click "Chat with document".
  </Step>
  <Step title="New Chat Opens">
    A new chat opens with the document attached.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ask Questions">
    Ask questions about the specific file or request a summary.
  </Step>
</Steps>


### Important Note

<Warning>
When you use this function, Realm answers are **only based on this specific file's content**. There will not be additional searches across your knowledge. To do a broader search, remove the file from the chat.
</Warning>

## Search History

Your full search history is available in the main menu, on the left side of your screen.

### Managing History

- View your recent searches in the sidebar
- Delete items by hovering over a result and clicking the "X" icon
- Click on any past search to run it again
