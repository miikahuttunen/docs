## RFP Automation with Realm

Realm can help you fill out RFPs, RFIs, and other information requests from customers. After an initial AI-generated draft, you can finalize the RFP together with your team.

<Note>
The file must be a spreadsheet (XLSX, XLS, or CSV).
</Note>

## How to Fill an RFP

<Steps>
  <Step title="Prepare Your File">
    Make sure you have an **unfilled** version of the RFP on your laptop.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload the RFP">
    Go to "RFPs" in Realm's main menu and click "Upload RFP".
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for Analysis">
    Wait for the file to be parsed and analyzed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the Plan">
    Review the AI-generated plan for filling each section and make edits if necessary.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select an Assistant">
    Choose which Assistant should fill each section—either Realm (full knowledge base) or a curated RFP Assistant.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill Sections">
    Click "Fill Section" for each section you want to fill with AI.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Reviewing the AI Plan

If the AI-generated plan looks off, you can:

- **Edit cells, rows, and columns** - Click on them to modify what should be filled
- **Add columns or rows** - Include additional fields in a section
- **Skip sections** - Exclude sections from AI filling

<Note>
The RFP will be broken down into sections that may not correspond to the sheets in the original spreadsheet. This is intentional—when you export the final RFP, the spreadsheet will still look exactly like the original.
</Note>

## Finalizing with Your Team

<Steps>
  <Step title="Review Each Answer">
    Go through every AI-generated answer and take one of these actions:
    - **Approve** - If it's correct and final
    - **Edit** - Modify the answer manually
    - **Follow-up** - Ask AI for edits, specification, or additions
    - **Assign** - Send to a colleague for review (they'll get an email and Slack notification)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ensure All Approved">
    Make sure every answer is reviewed and approved by you or a colleague.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Export">
    Once all answers are approved, export the RFP, give it a final check, and send to the customer.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Completed RFPs as Knowledge

<Tip>
Once you've approved all rows of an RFP, it's marked as "Completed" and automatically added to your knowledge base under a source called "RFPs". It will become part of the knowledge that Realm draws on for future queries and RFPs.
</Tip>

You can also curate which RFPs (or sections of an RFP) are added to a specific Agent's knowledge.
