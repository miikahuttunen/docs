## Confluence Connector

Connect Confluence to sync your wiki pages, blogs, and embedded files into Realm.

## Setting Up the Connection (Cloud)

<Steps>
  <Step title="Identify an Admin">
    Choose an admin with access to the Confluence spaces you want to sync.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Install Realm Connect App">
    Navigate to `https://[your-organization].withrealm.com/admin/connect?open=confluence` and follow the instructions:

    1. Go to [Realm on Atlassian Marketplace](https://marketplace.atlassian.com/apps/1233308/realm?tab=overview&hosting=cloud)
    2. Click "Get it now" and install the app for the site you want to connect
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure the App">
    Once installed, click "Configure" and copy the tokens into Realm.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/help-center/confluence-tokens.png" alt="Confluence token configuration" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Spaces">
    Choose which spaces you want to sync from the list of available spaces.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/help-center/confluence-spaces.png" alt="Confluence space selection" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Content Synced

From any Confluence Spaces you choose to sync:

- Pages
- Blogs
- Embedded files (such as PDFs)

## Content Not Supported

- Private Pages
- Comments

## Confluence Data Center

For Confluence Data Center (self-hosted), follow these steps:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create Personal Access Token">
    Follow [Atlassian's instructions](https://confluence.atlassian.com/enterprise/using-personal-access-tokens-1026032365.html) to create a PAT.

    <Note>
    Disable the "Automatic expiry" option to prevent sync interruptions.
    </Note>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter Credentials">
    Enter the token and your Confluence instance URL at `https://[your-organization].withrealm.com/admin/connect?open=confluence`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Spaces">
    Once connected, select the Confluence spaces you want to sync.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Smart Features

Realm integrates with Confluence's content lifecycle:

| Confluence Feature | Realm Behavior |
|-------------------|----------------|
| Verified pages | Auto-verified in Realm |
| Archived pages | Auto-hidden in Realm |
| Draft pages | Not shown until published |

## Best Practices

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Space Selection" icon="folder">
    Start with your most important spaces (product docs, engineering wiki) and expand as needed.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Use Confluence Verification" icon="badge-check">
    Mark important pages as verified in Confluence—they'll automatically be verified in Realm.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Archive Old Content" icon="box-archive">
    Archive outdated pages in Confluence rather than deleting them—they'll be hidden in Realm.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
