## Google Drive Connector

Realm supports two types of Google Drive syncs:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Shared Drives" icon="folder-tree">
    Admin-configured sync of team Shared Drives
  </Card>
  <Card title="Personal Drives" icon="user">
    User-configured sync of individual Drive access
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Connecting Shared Drives

### Setting Up the Connection

<Steps>
  <Step title="Identify an Admin">
    Choose a Google Drive admin to perform the connection.

    <Note>
    **Restricting API Access:**
    Realm only syncs the Shared Drives you select. To further limit API access:
    1. Create a Google Workspace account (e.g., realm@your-domain.com)
    2. Share only the Shared Drives you want Realm to access with this account
    3. Perform the OAuth flow with this account
    </Note>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect Drive">
    Navigate to `https://[your-organization].withrealm.com/admin/connect?open=google-drive` and click **Authorize**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Grant Access">
    You'll see an OAuth consent screen. Click **Allow**.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Shared Drives">
    Choose which Shared Drives you want to sync from the list of all available drives.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Content Synced

From selected Shared Drives, Realm syncs:

- Google Docs
- Google Slides
- Google Sheets
- PDFs
- Microsoft Office files (.docx, .pptx, .xlsx)
- And other common document formats

### Content Not Supported

- Google Forms
- Google Sites
- Comments on files

## Connecting Personal Drives

Every user can connect Google Drive files that they personally have access to.

### How to Connect

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings">
    Navigate to Settings in Realm.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect Google Drive">
    Find the Google Drive section and click Connect.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authorize">
    Complete the OAuth flow to grant Realm access.
  </Step>
</Steps>


### Permissions

- Other users who already had access to the same files will also gain access in Realm
- Realm strictly follows native Google Drive permissions for all materials
