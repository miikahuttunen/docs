## Zendesk Connector

Connect Zendesk to sync your support tickets and knowledge base into Realm.

## Setting Up the Connection

<Steps>
  <Step title="Identify a User">
    Choose a Zendesk user to set up the connection. The user should have access to the tickets you want to sync.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Realm">
    Navigate to `https://[your-organization].withrealm.com/admin/connect?open=zendesk`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create an API Key">
    Follow [Zendesk's instructions](https://support.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/4408889192858-Managing-API-token-access-to-the-Zendesk-API) to find or create your API key.

    Copy and paste the key into Realm.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter Your Domain">
    Enter your Zendesk domain (e.g., `https://company-name.zendesk.com/`).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter Your Email">
    Enter your Zendesk email address. This must be the same email you used to generate the API key.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit">
    Click Submit. Realm will start syncing your Zendesk tickets.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Sync Duration

The full sync can take from hours to multiple days, depending on how many tickets you have.

## Content Synced

- Support tickets
- Ticket conversations
- Knowledge base articles

## Best Practices

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="User Permissions" icon="shield">
    Use an account with appropriate access to all tickets you want to sync.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="API Key Security" icon="key">
    Keep your API key secure and consider creating a dedicated service account.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Monitor Sync Progress" icon="clock">
    Large ticket volumes may take several days to fully sync.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
