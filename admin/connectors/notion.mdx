## Notion Connector

Connect Notion to sync your pages and databases into Realm.

## Setting Up the Connection

<Steps>
  <Step title="Identify an Admin">
    Choose a Notion admin to perform the connection.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ensure Access">
    Make sure the admin has the necessary permissions to access the Notion data you want to sync.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect Notion">
    Navigate to `https://[your-organization].withrealm.com/admin/connect?open=notion` and click **Authorize Access to Notion**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete OAuth">
    You'll be redirected to Notion's OAuth flow.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Pages">
    At this point, you'll be asked to select which Pages to sync.

    <Note>
    Child pages are automatically included when you select a parent page.
    </Note>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete Setup">
    You'll be redirected back to Realm with the connection active.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Content Synced

- Pages
- Child pages (automatically included)
- Databases
- Database entries

## Permissions

Notion's permissions model is respected:
- Users can only see content they have access to in Notion
- Shared pages are accessible to all users with Notion access

## Best Practices

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Start with Key Workspaces" icon="folder">
    Select your most important top-level pages to sync their entire hierarchy.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Review Page Selection" icon="list-check">
    Remember that child pages are automatically included—no need to select them individually.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Admin Permissions" icon="shield">
    Ensure the connecting admin has access to all content you want to sync.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
