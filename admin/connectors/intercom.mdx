## Intercom Connector

Connect Intercom to sync your conversations and tickets into Realm.

<Note>
The Realm connector to Intercom is in **beta**.
</Note>

## Setting Up the Connection

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create an Intercom App">
    Visit the [Intercom Developer Hub](https://app.intercom.com/a/developer-signup) and create a new App.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy Access Token">
    After creating the app, copy the Access Token.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Realm">
    Navigate to `https://[your-organization].withrealm.com/admin/connect?open=intercom`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the Connector">
    Paste the access token into Realm and submit.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for Sync">
    Realm will start syncing your Intercom data.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Sync Duration

The full sync can take from hours to multiple days, depending on the volume of conversations and tickets.

## Content Synced

- Conversations
- Tickets
- Help center articles (if available)

## Best Practices

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Beta Considerations" icon="flask">
    As a beta feature, expect occasional updates and improvements. Report any issues to the Realm team.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Token Security" icon="key">
    Keep your access token secure and consider the permissions granted to the Intercom app.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Monitor Sync" icon="clock">
    Check the sync status periodically to ensure data is being updated.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
