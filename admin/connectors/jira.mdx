## Jira Connector

Connect Jira to sync your issues, epics, and project data into Realm.

## Setting Up the Connection

<Steps>
  <Step title="Identify an Admin">
    Choose an admin with access to the Jira projects you want to sync.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Install Realm Connect App">
    Navigate to `https://[your-organization].withrealm.com/admin/connect?open=jira` and follow the instructions:

    1. Go to [Realm on Atlassian Marketplace](https://marketplace.atlassian.com/apps/1233308/realm?tab=overview&hosting=cloud)
    2. Click "Get it now" and install the app for the site you want to connect
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure the App">
    Once installed, click "Configure" and copy the tokens into Realm.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Projects">
    Choose which projects you want to sync from the list of available projects.

  </Step>
</Steps>

## Content Synced

From the projects you choose to sync:

- Epics
- Issues
- Issue comments

## Jira Service Management

Realm's Jira connector can also be used to sync **Jira Service Management** data.

## Best Practices

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Project Selection" icon="folder">
    Start with your most active projects and expand coverage as needed.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Admin Access" icon="shield">
    Ensure the connecting admin has access to all projects you want to sync.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Token Security" icon="key">
    Keep your connection tokens secure and rotate them periodically.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
