## Slack Connector

This guide outlines the two main steps for connecting your Slack workspace to Realm:

1. **Connecting via OAuth** - Enables real-time syncing of new messages and files
2. **Importing Historical Data** - Upload a Slack workspace export to sync past conversations

## Part 1: Real-Time Connection

This process connects your Slack workspace to Realm, allowing continuous syncing of new messages and files from public channels.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Identify an Admin">
    Choose a Slack workspace administrator to perform the connection.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect Slack">
    Navigate to `https://[your-organization].withrealm.com/admin/connect?open=slack` and click **"Authorize access to Slack"**.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/help-center/slack-authorize.png" alt="Slack authorization" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Grant Access">
    You'll be redirected to a Slack OAuth consent screen. Review the permissions and click **Allow**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Once authorized, Realm will begin syncing new messages from **all public channels** in your workspace.

### Syncing Private Channels

To sync messages from **private channels**, add the Realm app to the channel you want to sync.

## Part 2: Historical Data Import

Due to Slack API limitations, a full historical sync via the API is slow (~900 messages per hour). Uploading a workspace export is the recommended method to quickly import your entire message history.

### Export Your Slack Data

<Steps>
  <Step title="Access Workspace Settings">
    As a Slack workspace admin, go to **Settings & administration** > **Workspace settings**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Navigate to Export">
    Click **Import/Export Data** in the top right, then select the **Export** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure Export">
    Choose a date range for your export. To get all history, select the entire range.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start Export">
    Click **Start Export**. Slack will notify you when the export is complete.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Download">
    Download the ZIP file containing your workspace data.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Upload to Realm

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Slack Settings">
    Navigate to `https://[your-organization].withrealm.com/admin/connect?open=slack`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload Export">
    Find the historical data import section and upload the ZIP file.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/help-center/slack-upload.png" alt="Slack export upload" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for Processing">
    A progress bar will appear. Processing time depends on the size of your export.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Important Considerations

- **Private Channels**: You can choose to exclude private channel messages using a toggle in settings
- **File Size Limit**: Maximum 10GB. For larger exports, use multiple exports with different date ranges

## Content Synced

### By Default

- Conversations in public channels (messages, thread replies)
- Files shared in public channels

### Additional Content (Optional)

- **Slack Connect channels** - Configurable in settings
- **Private channels** - Any channels where Realm's Slack App has been added
- **DMs** - Not self-serve; contact us if interested

### Not Supported

- Canvases
- Lists

## Notes

Slack can be a noisy source. To reduce noise, Realm:

- Ignores threads with bot messages and no human messages
- Penalizes archived channels in ranking
- Penalizes very short messages in ranking
