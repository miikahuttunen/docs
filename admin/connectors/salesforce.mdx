## Salesforce Connector

Connect Salesforce to sync your CRM data into Realm.

## Setting Up the Connection

<Steps>
  <Step title="Identify an Admin">
    Choose a Salesforce admin to perform the connection.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ensure Access">
    Make sure the admin has full read access to the following Salesforce objects:
    - Account
    - Opportunity
    - Contact
    - Contract
    - Order
    - Case
    - Campaign
    - Lead
    - Product
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect Salesforce">
    Navigate to `https://[your-organization].withrealm.com/admin/connect?open=salesforce` and click **Authorize**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete OAuth">
    You'll be redirected to Salesforce's OAuth flow. Log in if needed, then allow access for Realm.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Properties and Objects">
    After being redirected back to Realm, select which properties and objects you want to sync.

  </Step>
</Steps>

## Content Synced

Only the data you explicitly select is synced and available in Realm:

- **Accounts** - Company information
- **Opportunities** - Sales pipeline data
- **Contacts** - People at accounts
- **Contracts** - Agreement details
- **Orders** - Purchase records
- **Cases** - Support tickets
- **Campaigns** - Marketing campaigns
- **Leads** - Prospective customers
- **Products** - Product catalog

## Configuration Options

For each object, you can select:

- Which fields/properties to sync
- Whether to include related records
- Custom objects (if available)

## Best Practices

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Required Permissions" icon="key">
    The connecting user must have read access to all objects and fields being synced.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Custom Objects" icon="cube">
    If you use custom objects, ensure they're included in your sync configuration.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Field Selection" icon="list">
    Focus on fields that contain searchable, useful content rather than system fields.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
