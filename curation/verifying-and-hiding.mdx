## Why Verify and Hide Content?

> "I have no idea if this document is still up-to-date."

This is one of the most frequent pains that Realm users experience. It's impossible to know what company knowledge to trust—and if people can't trust knowledge, they won't use it.


Realm's verification and hiding features are designed to solve this:

- **Verifying** a source tells the rest of the team that a source can be trusted—and means it will rank higher in Realm
- **Hiding** a source ensures that outdated or misleading information isn't accidentally used by your colleagues

Your whole team is empowered to help build trust in your company's most valuable resources.

## Verifying Content

### What Verification Does

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Visual Indicator" icon="badge-check">
    The document is shown with a green badge, letting other users know it's a trustworthy source
  </Card>
  <Card title="Search Boost" icon="arrow-up">
    The document is boosted in Realm's search results and more likely to be used as a source for answers
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

### What to Verify

Examples of documents worth verifying:

- Key pieces of company documentation (wiki articles with basic company info and processes)
- Latest versions of contract templates, sales decks, competitive battle cards
- Key customer-facing documentation (product roadmap, help center, trust center)
- Official policies and procedures

## Hiding Content

### What Hiding Does

Hidden results will not be returned by Realm or any Agent:

- They can't be found in search results
- They will not be used as sources in answers

<Warning>
Only hide results that you can confidently say shouldn't be used for any purpose.
</Warning>

### What to Hide

Examples of documents worth hiding:

- Older versions and drafts of customer contracts
- Outdated versions of important documents (product specs, help center articles, internal guidelines)
- Documents that may confuse Realm (e.g., spreadsheets with mock Q&A used to evaluate the product)

## Managing Curations

All verified and hidden sources can be found under **"Your curation"** in Realm's main menu.

### Viewing Curations

The tabs at the top let you toggle between:
- **Your curations** - Documents you've personally verified or hidden
- **All curations** - All curations in your workspace

### Best Practices

<Tip>
It's good practice to periodically review your verified and hidden sources since your company's knowledge will evolve over time.
</Tip>

## How to Verify or Hide

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the Document">
    Locate the document in Search results or Chat citations.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Actions Menu">
    Click the menu icon or right-click on the document.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Action">
    Choose "Verify" to mark as trusted, or "Hide" to remove from results.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Your curation is saved and takes effect immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Impact on Search and Chat

| Action | Search Results | Chat Sources | AI Agents |
|--------|---------------|--------------|-----------|
| **Verified** | Boosted ranking | Preferred source | Higher priority |
| **Hidden** | Not shown | Not used | Excluded |
| **Neither** | Normal ranking | Normal priority | Normal access |
