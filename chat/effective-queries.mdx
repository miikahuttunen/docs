## How to Write Effective Chat Queries

The way you prompt Chat has a big impact on the quality of the answers. A good rule of thumb: ask questions the same way you would ask a knowledgeable colleague—clear, specific, and with enough context.

## Best Practices

### 1. Mention Good Sources

If you know of a relevant source, include it in your query.

<Tip>
**Good:** "Based on our shareholders' agreement, how often should we hold board meetings?"
</Tip>

### 2. Ask Follow-up Questions

Continue the conversation to dig deeper into a topic.

```text
First query: "What is the current list of Realm connectors?"
Follow-up: "Do we have Gong on the roadmap?"
```

### 3. Specify Output Format

Provide instructions on how you want your reply formatted.

<Tip>
**Good examples:**
- "Create a table describing how Realm differs from Glean"
- "Write a short email about our HIPAA and GDPR compliance"
</Tip>

### 4. Specify Tools and Time Ranges

Be explicit about where to look and when.

```text
"Based on Linear and Slack, what's the status of Realm API project?"
"List all PRs about the Cycles feature from the past two days"
"What are the most recent mentions of bugs on #engineering-product?"
```

### 5. Request Corrections

If the initial answer got something wrong, ask for corrections.

```text
"Only include results from Acme company, exclude everything else."
"There should be more clients listed. Can you double check?"
```

## Less Effective Prompts

<Warning>
Avoid vague or incomplete prompts like:
- "Connectors?"
- "Roadmap?"
- "Email about GDPR."

These lack context and specificity, leading to less accurate responses.
</Warning>

## When to Ask Follow-ups vs. Start a New Chat

You can ask follow-up questions, and Chat will remember the context from your previous message. This helps you dig deeper without repeating yourself.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Ask a follow-up" icon="arrow-right">
    When you're continuing the same topic—for example, clarifying or expanding on the previous answer.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Start a new thread" icon="plus">
    When you're switching to a different topic—this avoids confusion and keeps responses relevant.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Using the @ Keybind

`@` is a universal keybind in Realm that lets you quickly select Assistants, documents, or tools.

For example, to switch to GPT-4.1:
1. Type `@`
2. Start typing "gpt"
3. Press Enter to select GPT-4.1
