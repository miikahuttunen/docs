## Automatic Filter Detection

Realm is trained to automatically apply filters that you refer to in your chat query. You can mention specific criteria, and Realm will apply the appropriate filters.

## How It Works

When you include filter criteria in your query, Realm automatically detects and applies them:

```text
"How many HubSpot deals of type new business are in the stage building business case?"
```

Realm will:
1. Identify the data source (HubSpot)
2. Apply the deal type filter (new business)
3. Apply the stage filter (building business case)
4. Return relevant results


## Reviewing and Editing Filters

You can see what filters Realm chose to apply. If some of them are incorrect, you can:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Review Applied Filters">
    Check the filters shown above your results to see what was applied.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit Filters">
    Click on the pencil icon to modify any incorrect filters.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Rerun Query">
    After editing, rerun the query to get updated results.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Tips for Effective Filtering

<Tip>
Be specific in your queries to help Realm apply the right filters:
- Mention specific tools (Slack, HubSpot, Linear)
- Include time ranges (past week, last month)
- Name specific channels, folders, or entities
- Specify document types when relevant
</Tip>
